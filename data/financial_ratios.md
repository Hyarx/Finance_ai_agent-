# Financial Ratios Interpretation

## Profitability
- ROE > 15%: excellent
- ROE 5-15%: acceptable
- ROE < 5%: weak

## Valuation
- P/E < 15: potentially undervalued
- P/E 15-25: fair valuation
- P/E > 25: potentially overvalued

## Debt
- Debt/Equity < 1: low leverage
- Debt/Equity 1-2: moderate leverage
- Debt/Equity > 2: high leverage, risky

## Liquidity
- Current Ratio > 2: very liquid
- Current Ratio 1-2: acceptable
- Current Ratio < 1: liquidity risk

## Profitability margins
- Net Margin > 20%: excellent
- Net Margin 10-20%: good
- Net Margin < 10%: low
