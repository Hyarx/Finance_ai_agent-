# Audit Red Flags (ISA Standards)

## Financial Red Flags
- Altman Z-Score below 1.8: bankruptcy risk
- Current ratio below 1: liquidity problem
- Debt/Equity above 3: excessive leverage
- Negative net margin two years in a row
- Revenue growth inconsistent with sector

## Non-Financial Red Flags
- Unexpected management rotation
- Auditor change without clear reason
- Ongoing lawsuits or regulatory investigations
- Significant off-balance sheet commitments
- Aggressive accounting policies

## ISA Standards Reference
- ISA 240: auditor responsibilities regarding fraud
- ISA 315: identifying risks of material misstatement
- ISA 570: going concern assessment
